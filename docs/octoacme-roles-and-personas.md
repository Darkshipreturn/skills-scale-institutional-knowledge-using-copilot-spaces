# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers validate whether product changes meet acceptance criteria, quality standards, and release readiness. They partner closely with developers and product stakeholders to ensure risk is identified early and resolved before launch.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test strategy for features, regressions, and critical workflows
- Validate acceptance criteria against expected user and business outcomes
- Identify quality risks, defects, and release blockers
- Collaborate with developers on testability, automation opportunities, and bug triage
- Communicate test status, known risks, and go/no-go readiness to project leads

### Goals
- Increase confidence in release quality and customer experience
- Reduce surprise defects and production incidents
- Ensure quality is considered throughout delivery, not only at the end

### Typical Communication
- Sprint or release readiness reviews
- Bug and defect triage with engineering
- QA signoff and status updates with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on test coverage, defect reproduction, and remediation
- Works with Product Managers to clarify expected behavior, edge cases, and acceptance criteria
- Works with Project Managers to surface test dependencies, readiness, and risk for milestones and releases

---

## Design / UX Researchers

### Role Summary
Design / UX Researchers focus on customer needs, accessibility, usability, and the user experience of the solution. They help translate customer feedback and research findings into design decisions and product improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer and user insights through research, interviews, and usability testing
- Define user flows, accessibility requirements, and interaction patterns
- Validate design decisions against stated problem statements and desired outcomes
- Partner with product and engineering on trade-offs and implementation feasibility
- Document design recommendations and usability findings for the team

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer value
- Reduce rework caused by weak or unvalidated product assumptions
- Ensure user-centered decisions shape delivery priorities

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery sessions and design reviews
- Research findings and usability test summaries
- Collaboration in roadmap and backlog discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on prioritization, outcomes, and customer need validation
- Works with Developers to align implementation with design intent and accessibility standards
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, design handoffs, and dependency planning

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects provide technical direction, system-level thinking, and design guidance. They help teams make sound engineering decisions while balancing delivery speed, quality, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture patterns, integration approaches, and technical standards
- Guide technical trade-offs, sequencing, and risk reduction decisions
- Review solution design for scalability, reliability, maintainability, and security
- Support developers in problem solving and design escalation
- Provide dependency and technical risk updates to project stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve system quality and long-term maintainability
- Reduce architecture drift and integration risk
- Support reliable delivery without over-engineering the solution

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision discussions and design reviews
- Technical dependency and risk updates
- Cross-team planning for integration and platform concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers on technical feasibility, trade-offs, and sequencing implications
- Supports Developers with standards, reviews, and technical decision-making
- Helps Project Managers estimate delivery risk, dependencies, and milestones more accurately

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads focus on safe delivery, service reliability, deployment readiness, and post-deployment recovery. They help ensure that changes reach production with appropriate checks and visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning, deployment windows, and rollback readiness
- Define observability, monitoring, and operational checks for production changes
- Support smoke testing, staging validation, and incident readiness
- Partner with engineering and QA on launch criteria and release communication
- Help triage production issues and coordinate mitigation actions

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and operational disruption
- Improve release predictability and incident response readiness
- Ensure product changes are observable and recoverable in production

### Typical Communication
- Release gating and deployment reviews
- Incident coordination and operational updates
- Stakeholder and support communication during launches

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm deployment readiness, rollback plans, and release criteria
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer impact, launch communication, and expected outcomes
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder updates

---

## Business / Domain Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Business / Domain Stakeholders provide domain expertise, requirement context, and decision-making input from the business side. They help ensure the team is solving the right problem and the delivered work creates value.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business goals, constraints, and domain-specific requirements
- Review solution direction against business needs and priorities
- Provide input on trade-offs, customer scenarios, and strategic context
- Approve important milestones, scope decisions, and readiness criteria when needed
- Surface issues that affect business outcomes, customer experience, or compliance

### Goals
- Ensure the team delivers outcomes that matter to the business and customers
- Improve alignment between strategy, delivery, and customer impact
- Support informed decision-making throughout the lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Planning workshops and stakeholder check-ins
- Product reviews and milestone approvals
- Business impact updates and issue escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to define requirements, success metrics, and trade-offs
- Provides business validation to Developers and QA on expected user flows and acceptance conditions
- Works with Project Managers to align priorities, approvals, decisions, and communication timelines

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams may choose a subset of these roles depending on project size, maturity, and criticality; one person may hold more than one role when needed.
